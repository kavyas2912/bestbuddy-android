Sound triggers & timing
Contacts & call escalation
Beeper & voice prompts
Uses the phone’s alarm volume and respects Android audio restrictions. Stop or I’m OK cancels the beeper. Test safely before use.
Loud sound → immediate voice check → two repeated checks → final 30-second warning → family call attempt → selected wait → final 30-second warning → healthcare call attempt. I’m OK, spoken “Buddy, I am okay”, or activity inside Buddy cancels escalation. Existing calls delay the next attempt. Short/public emergency services require the system dialer; use a full healthcare contact number. Unlocking the phone or activity in Buddy cancels escalation; other apps’ touches are not tracked. The Try button never places calls. If automatic calling is off, escalation stays on this phone.
Read safety limits
Loud-sound analysis runs only on this phone with a visible background notification. No audio is saved or sent. It may react to TV/music and miss incidents; it does not detect falls or medical emergencies. Android, battery restrictions or microphone conflicts can interrupt monitoring.
